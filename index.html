Platform context: RSEA Safety is migrating its B2B customer portal to Adobe Commerce (Magento). The Uniform Management System (UMS) is the largest custom module — covering employee entitlements, embroidery/heat press configuration, logo libraries, placement diagrams, and digital proof approval. Companies can be onboarded with or without UMS depending on their needs. 64 features across 8 capability buckets. Key differentiators: compliance document hub, budget & approval engine, UMS, predictive reorder, and APL management.
